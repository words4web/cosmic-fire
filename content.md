Complete assessment on the function and condition of your fire doors
Most commercial and rented buildings in London have many fire doors, all of which can be damaged due to everyday wear and tear or vandalism, if these doors are found to be either non functioning or non-compliant you may be liable in the event of a fire.

We will perform a complete check on all the doors in your building to ensure they are compliant with all the relevant regulations and provide certification on an annual basis.

BM Trada Q Mark scheme fully certified inspectors
All our fire door inspectors are fully BM Trada Q Mark Certified to ensure the risk assessment of your fire doors is carried out competently and prepare a detailed report and survey on the function and condition of your fire doors.

London Fire Door installation
Correct fire door Installation London
A fire door assembly includes the door leaf, frame, doorstops , seals, hinges and hardware. If just one wrong component is used it may effect the performance of the fire door which can cost lives.
Installation of fire doors is a complex procedure requiring detailed understanding, our fully qualified installers have the skills and knowledge required to install and certify one or all the doors in your building.

Fire door Installation you can trust
There is a legal requirement for manufacturers of fire doors to adhere to a certain standard, but if they are not installed properly the performance can be severely compromised.
All of our installers are certified under the BM Trada Q mark fire door installation scheme which provides reassurance that the products specified have been installed correctly and will perform to the required fire resistance in the event of a fire.

Seals and Ironmongery
Correct fire door installation includes making sure the seals and Ironmongery items that are used on fire doors including locks, latches overhead door closures and hinges are all third party certified products using the wrong parts can seriously affect how the door performs.
We include a detailed specification on all the fixtures and fittings used on our certified fire doors.

London Fire Door maintenance
Do fire doors need maintenance?
There is a legal requirement for those responsible for buildings under the Regulatory Reform (Fire Safety) Order to ensure that all fire doors continue to retain the ability to resist the passage of fire throughout their working life.

If issues are found with your fire doors they will need correcting as soon as possible using the correct components, failure to do so will invalidate certification.

However, a doorset’s fire resistance capabilities can be seriously compromised if it is not maintained by an individual who has appropriate knowledge of this complex life safety product

What is fire stopping?
Fire stopping is the sealing of any openings in order to prevent smoke, heat and fire from spreading through buildings, we create fire resistant sections within buildings using a variety of fire resistant materials that when combined restrict the spread of fire effectively. As each building is different, the combination of products needed to stop fire spreading must be specific to the building. We create a complete assessment of your building with our recommendations and timescales needed to achieve an effective fire stopping solution.
